Nintendo Entertainment System Game Pak cartridge, 1985, real size 12.0 x 2.0 x 13.5 cm, made of plastic. Tall rectangular gray cartridge with stepped shoulders, vertical grip ridges along the upper back, recessed rectangular front label area, NINTENDO molded into the rear shell, small screws on the back and a wide exposed edge-connector slot along the bottom; individual game variants change only the label text and neutral title treatment
