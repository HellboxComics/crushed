Nintendo Game Boy DMG-01 handheld, 1989, real size 9.0 x 3.2 x 14.8 cm, made of plastic. Tall light-gray handheld with gray-green rectangular LCD window, NINTENDO GAME BOY text beneath it, black cross D-pad, two magenta A and B buttons, gray SELECT and START buttons, speaker grille at lower right, power switch on top and cartridge slot across the rear; bottom has headphone jack and sides carry contrast and volume wheels
