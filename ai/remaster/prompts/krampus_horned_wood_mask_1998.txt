Hand-carved horned Krampus-style wooden mask, circa 1998. Heavy carved mask with dark brown weathered face, deep eye sockets, exaggerated brows, long carved nose, red-painted tongue and two large swept horns fixed above the forehead, front heavily textured by chisel marks, sides drilled for leather straps, rear hollowed to fit a face, top dominated by horn mounts and bottom ending in beard-like carved ridges
