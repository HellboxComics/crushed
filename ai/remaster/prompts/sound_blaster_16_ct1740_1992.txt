Creative Labs Sound Blaster 16 CT1740 ISA sound card, 1992. Long green ISA expansion card with CREATIVE LABS and SOUND BLASTER 16 identification, black integrated circuits, rows of capacitors and resistors, gold ISA edge connector along the bottom and a silver rear mounting bracket carrying joystick/MIDI, line, microphone and speaker jacks; rear face is solder points and traces with a metal retention tab at one end
