Beistle jointed cardboard skeleton decoration, circa 1995, real size 40.0 x 0.3 x 150.0 cm, made of card. Flat white printed cardboard skeleton assembled from separate skull, torso, arm and leg sections joined by small brass paper fasteners so the limbs can swing, with BEISTLE identification and black bone-line printing; when stored it folds awkwardly into a stack of overlapping limbs
