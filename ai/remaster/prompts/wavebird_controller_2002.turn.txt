Nintendo WaveBird Wireless Controller, 2002, real size 14.0 x 10.0 x 6.5 cm, made of plastic. Light-gray wireless controller sharing the GameCube controller silhouette with large green A button, red B button, X/Y controls, yellow C-stick, gray analog stick, D-pad and shoulder triggers, but with no cable; center body is thicker to hold batteries and wireless electronics, with power switch and channel selector
